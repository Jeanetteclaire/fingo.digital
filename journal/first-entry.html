I'm not sure anyone still reads these days . . . maybe I would be better off making a video . . . but for now it's words on this webpage.

So what am I writing about and why document it at all?

I'm writing mostly about things I have learned and observations I have made as I navigate the AI/coding/building world we currently live in . . . especially as a beginner, newbie . . . returning to the computer club kid I was . . . (even if it was over 40 years ago).

I am documenting mostly for me, so in the future I can look back on my ZX Spectrum version of builds, and (hopefully) feel affection for the primitive, messy, delightful things I managed to get together despite very little knowledge. To see how far I have come. From writing my first error-free git command to . . . well who knows, I can't know what I'm not aware of . . . but I'm curious to find out.

If you find it funny or educational and want to stick around — welcome, I'm Jeanette, glad to meet you.
