Starting fingo.digital

I see so much stuff built on Insta and TikTok, amazing stuff and clever stuff but stuff built by people who know what they are doing. Maybe that’s what most people want to see but I’m not that person, I want to see the the messy uncomfortable struggle of trying to build things when the learning curve is so steep you are probably gonna fall of backwards at some point. That’s my journey . . .  .. and if you are someone like me, someone who doesn’t see boundaries and doesn’t care what they know or don’t know because they are gonna figure it out and built what they want to anyway  . . . . maybe this webpage is for you. 

You can see what I built, and what I learned. It’s all pretty basic and definitely obvious for any professional dev but for me it’s magic . . . what the rights letters and numbers in a particular order can create will never stop delighting me.

So if you want to watch someone go from “I don’t know how to code” to “I shipped a thing” and maybe do the same yourself, tag along . . . at least you can learn from my (many) mistakes or at least have laugh at them . .

fingo.digital — built by hand, documented honestly.
