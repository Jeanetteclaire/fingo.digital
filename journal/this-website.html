Starting fingo.digital


I see so much stuff built on Insta and TikTok, amazing stuff and clever stuff but stuff built by people who know what they are doing. Maybe that’s what most people want to see but I’m not that person, I want to see the the messy uncomfortable struggle of trying to build things when the learning curve is so steep you are probably gonna fall of backwards at some point. That’s my journey . . .  .. and if you are someone like me someone who doesn’t see boundaries and doesn’t care what they know or dont know because they are gonna figure it out and built what they want to maybe this webpage is for you. 

You can see what I built, and what I learned. It’s all pretty basic and probably banal for any professional dev but for me it’s magic . . . that the rights letters and numbers in a particular order can create will never stop delighting me.
