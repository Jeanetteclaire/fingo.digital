SVG

learningdiscovery

I fell in love . . . with a parallax scroll effect . . . so much so that I had to try to build one. Which is a journey I am still on . . . but what I learned first was that I needed to make an SVG . . . an SV what I thought? Up until now my life has been all about finding or creating PNGs . . . SVGs were never a thing.

For those of you who don't know (but would like to) SVG is a Scalable Vector Graphic — not made of pixels, instead it uses mathematical formulae to draw shapes . . . and is therefore infinitely scalable without becoming blurry. Ta dah!!! Razor sharp shapes!

Also animatable with CSS or JavaScript . . . if you know how.
