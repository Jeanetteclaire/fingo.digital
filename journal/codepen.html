Codepen

toolsdiscovery

For anyone out there who loves a creative challenge and is looking for community (ie me last week) check out codepen.io. Claude suggested it to me and it was a revelation (I mean I know he wasn't gatekeeping it but it defo felt like it!)

So! Much! Inspiration! So many amazing things people have built and all the code behind it so you can understand how they built what they did.

I use the free plan because I'm not actually good enough to upload anything yet . . . but I can at least try the challenges in a safe secret non-publishing kind of way. As for submitting one day . . . who knows?

Watch this space!
